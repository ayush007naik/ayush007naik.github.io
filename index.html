Multiverse City

One city. Multiple possibilities.

Multiverse City is a local-commerce and services ecosystem designed to connect customers with local businesses and everyday services through one unified platform. The vision is simple: make local discovery and transactions feel as easy as using a single super-app.

Five apps are now live on Google Play and operating in Mandi today. The full city-wide launch is coming soon — expected by January 2027.

Problem + target

Local commerce is fragmented across separate shops, booking tools, listings and delivery apps. Multiverse City is designed for customers who want one place to discover and transact, and for local businesses that need shared digital workflows.

Business model

A unified local platform: customers get one place to discover and transact with local businesses, while vendors and service providers plug into shared dashboards and workflows. The commercial model is being validated; no revenue or user metrics are claimed here.
